This policy explains how information may be handled when you use ASUNCION CHESS 3D, a chess game that lets players practice and play against a computer opponent.

Information processed by the app or its service providers may be used to deliver the game, maintain security, diagnose errors, and support the platform. This policy does not mean that the developer receives all information processed by third-party providers.

The developer does not sell personal information collected directly through the game. Service providers may process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

6. Data retention and security

Information stored or processed by third-party providers is subject to their retention and security practices. Reasonable care is taken in maintaining the app, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

The game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact the developer using the project's GitHub repository.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its features change. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
